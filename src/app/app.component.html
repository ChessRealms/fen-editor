FEN Editor

Choose a piece or Erase, then tap or paint squares. With Move, drag a piece or tap its square and then a destination. Release on the board to apply; release outside or press Escape to cancel.

Keyboard: Tab enters or leaves the board. Arrow keys move focus; Home/End jump to the row edges, Ctrl+Home/End to the board corners. Enter/Space uses the selected tool. Delete/Backspace erases the focused square. Escape cancels selection.
